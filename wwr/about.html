Our purpose is to bring adventure and joy through white water rafting. Our mission is safety, fun, and much more wisdom as you explore. Thanks.

History

Founded in 2005, Kings And Priests Rafting Company started with a single raft and a passion for the river. Over the years, we've grown into a trusted adventure company known for safety, fun, and unforgettable memories.

Today, we continue that legacy by offering world-class rafting experiences for families, friends, and thrill-seekers from around the world.
